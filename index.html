THE STORY OF THE SURVIVORS

The day the world went dark, and the ones who refused to die.

Chapter One — The Night It All Fell

No one remembers exactly when it started. One night, without warning, the mobs stopped following the rules. Spiders crawled out of the caves in endless waves. Creepers came in silence, their hiss the only warning. The great settlements — built over hundreds of hours — fell one by one, drowned in a tide of monsters no sword could stop.

The servers went quiet. Bases were abandoned. Chat turned to static. For a while, it looked like the world was just... gone.

"I remember standing on the roof of our base, watching the last torch go out. I told myself: if I die, I die. But if I live, I'm coming back for everyone."

Chapter Two — Swafts, The One Who Wouldn't Die

While everyone else logged off, Swafts stayed. Alone. Underground. Three hearts left, one iron sword, and a half-broken shield. For nine nights straight he dug — not to hide, but to build something the mobs couldn't reach and no one else had dared to make: a fortress carved into the heart of a mountain, sealed with a single obsidian door.

He didn't just survive. He started bringing people in. One by one, the few who had made it — scattered, half-starved players with nothing but their will to live — found their way to his beacon. Swafts gave them walls, food, and weapons. And slowly, the whispers began.

The Noonblade was real. A blade that could cut through the darkness itself. Legend said it had been lost in the collapse. Swafts never said where he found it. He only ever said: "We're not done yet."

Chapter Three — The Rebuild

The survivors banded together and pooled everything they had left. They built a new spawn — not a monument to a fallen world, but a promise of the next one. Farmlands that never sleep. A marketplace for the traders who dare the roads. Walls lit brighter than the old world ever was.

The mobs still come. They always will. But now, when a creeper hisses in the dark, it doesn't end in silence anymore — it ends with a team of survivors answering back.

This is the Noonblade SMP. The world that was supposed to end. And the people who said no.
