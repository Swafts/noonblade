FAQ

Quick answers to the questions everyone asks.

What Minecraft version do I need?

Any recent Java Edition version. The server runs on modern versions and stays up to date with the community.

Is it pay-to-win?

No. Nothing on the server is sold — no ranks, no crates, no kits behind payments. Everyone plays on equal ground.

Are hostile mobs disabled?

Hostile mob spawns are managed by the server to keep the world calm and your builds undamaged. Deaths are still possible — it's survival, after all.

Is griefing allowed?

No. The server runs on trust and a small, mature community. Staff handle any issues that do come up.

Can I record or stream on the server?

Absolutely — the community loves it. Share your content in the Discord and it may even be featured on the channel.

How do I get whitelisted / approved?

Just join the Discord, say hi, and the staff will get you set up. The world is open to players who respect the community.
